# Governance

## Purpose

Project Watt Cubed exists to produce a completely free-software voxel game,
renderer and ecosystem. Decisions should preserve users' ability to study,
modify, redistribute, sell and host the project.

## Permanent principles

1. Official project-owned software remains under strong copyleft.
2. No proprietary licensing exception or proprietary distribution
   dual-licence will be issued for project-owned components.
3. Contributors retain copyright; the project does not require assignment.
4. Proprietary dependencies may not become mandatory.
5. Official network services and official mods must make corresponding source
   available under the project's policies.
6. Weakening copyleft requires permission from every affected copyright holder,
   not merely a maintainer vote.
7. Trademark control may prevent confusion but must not prevent truthful
   description of a fork's origin or compatibility.
8. Security fixes may be held under a proportionate, temporary embargo, then
   published under the normal licence.
9. Technical authority is exercised through public review, reproducible
   evidence and documented decisions.
10. Maintainers remain accountable and removable through the process below.

## Maintainers

Maintainers review changes, manage releases and project infrastructure, and
apply these rules. They do not receive ownership of contributors' copyrights
or authority to issue proprietary exceptions.

Maintainer additions and removals use a public governance proposal with at
least 14 days for comment. A proposal passes with a two-thirds vote of active,
non-conflicted maintainers. A maintainer does not vote on their own removal. If
that leaves fewer than two eligible voters, the proposal requires majority
support from the human contributors listed in `CONTRIBUTORS.md` who have made a
substantive contribution during the preceding twelve months. The rationale and
result are recorded publicly.

This interim process should be revisited when the project moves into a
multi-maintainer organisation, but the permanent principles above continue to
apply.

## Decisions and conflicts

Normal technical decisions use consensus where practical. If consensus fails,
maintainers record the alternatives, evidence and decision in a public issue or
pull request. Anyone with a financial, employment or personal conflict must
disclose it and abstain where it could reasonably affect the decision.

Nothing in this document overrides the rights already granted by an applicable
free-software or free-content licence.
