# Trademark policy

This is a permissive policy for the names “Project Watt Cubed” and
“voxel_engine”, and for official Project Watt Cubed logos and visual identity
(collectively, the project marks). It protects users from confusion without
restricting the copyright-licence freedoms.

This policy does not claim that every mark is registered, and it does not
replace rights or exceptions available under applicable law.

## Uses that are welcome

You may:

- truthfully say that software is based on, compatible with, or derived from
  Project Watt Cubed;
- discuss, review, teach or document the project;
- run a clearly identified fan or community site;
- redistribute an unmodified official release using its official name; and
- describe a fork's historical origin after giving the fork a distinct name
  and branding.

Attribution must not imply sponsorship, endorsement or official status.

## Uses that require permission

Ask the project maintainers before using a project mark:

- as the primary name or logo of a modified distribution, product, company or
  service;
- in merchandise or marketing in a way that suggests official origin; or
- for a domain, account or package name likely to be mistaken for official
  infrastructure.

Permission should be granted on fair, non-discriminatory terms when confusion
is unlikely.

## Uses that are not permitted

Do not:

- present an unofficial or incompatible fork as an official release;
- call an unofficial registry, server list or service “official”;
- imply endorsement by the project or its maintainers;
- impersonate the project, its maintainers or official servers; or
- retain official branding in a materially modified distribution when that
  branding would mislead users about compatibility, provenance or support.

You may always make truthful statements necessary to identify the original
project and explain compatibility. Removing official branding does not remove
any copyright-licence obligation.
